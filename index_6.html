What We Built

Full-featured Minecraft-like experience — engineered in Luau, built for Roblox.

01
Open World Gen
Procedural voxel terrain with forests, lakes, caves, and multiple biomes — every world is unique.
02
Combat & Survival
Hostile mobs, day-night cycles, hunger system, and PvP. Survive or be eliminated.

Block Building
Hundreds of block types. Build anything from a cottage to a massive fortress.
